Delete your ChiLaxN account

App: ChiLaxN · Last updated: October 6, 2026

You can delete your ChiLaxN account and the data linked to it at any time. Choose whichever option suits you.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open ChiLaxN and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and saved titles are removed immediately and cannot be recovered.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Use this if you can no longer open the app, or have uninstalled it.

  1. Email us from the same address you registered with.
  2. Use the subject Account deletion request and include the name on your account.
  3. We may reply to confirm it is you. We will delete your account and data within 30 days, and confirm by email when it is done.

Email a deletion request

Or write to chilaxnappforyou@gmail.com

What may remain

  • Aggregate search counts used for the Trending list. They are not linked to your name or email.
  • Copies in routine backups of our provider, which are overwritten on a rolling basis. Deleted data is not restored or used.
  • Information we are required to keep by law.

Used the app without an account?

Guest data is tied to a random identifier that we cannot connect to you. To remove your saved titles, create an account in the app and then delete it using Option 1.
