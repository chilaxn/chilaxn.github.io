ChiLaxN Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

ChiLaxN ("we", "us", "the app") is a movie, TV series and anime discovery app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Appwrite, and are not readable by us.

Guest identifier. If you use the app without an account, a random anonymous identifier is created so your saved titles can be stored. It does not contain your name or email.

Saved titles. The movies, TV series and anime you save as favorites are stored against your account or guest identifier.

Search activity. When you search for a movie, the search text and the top matching title may be recorded as aggregate counts, which power the "Trending" list. These counts are not used to build a profile of you.

Information stored on your device. Your selected watch region and your "Continue Exploring" history are stored only on your phone. We do not receive them.

We do not collect your contacts, microphone, camera, messages, precise location or health information.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your saved titles and keep them available if you change phones.
  • To show where titles are available in the region you chose.
  • To calculate trending titles and improve discovery.

3. Third-party services

  • Appwrite provides account sign-in and stores account and saved-title data.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) provides titles, ratings, posters and other metadata. Your device requests this data directly. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • JustWatch, through TMDB, provides streaming, rental and purchase availability. Availability varies by country and can change at any time.

These services process data under their own privacy policies.

4. What ChiLaxN does not do

ChiLaxN does not host, stream or provide downloads of movies or TV shows. Where we show watch options, they link to legitimate services.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the services listed above only as needed to run the app, or when the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and saved titles until you delete your account. You can delete it inside the app (Profile, then Delete Account) or by email. See how to delete your account and data, including what is removed and what may remain.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Appwrite. No online service can promise absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children

ChiLaxN is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing us. You can also clear your on-device history by uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, such as adding advertising, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.

11. Contact
